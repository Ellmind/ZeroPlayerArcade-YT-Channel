Zero Player Arcade

A YouTube channel of computer-controlled arcade fights.

Zero Player Arcade is a YouTube channel where nobody holds a controller. Every fight is played by the computer in the open-source Ikemen GO engine: CPU against CPU in the CPU LEAGUE series, and a self-learning fighter in the AI CLIMB series.

About this application

The Zero Player Arcade application is a private publishing tool. The channel owner runs it on their own computer to manage the Zero Player Arcade channel through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. It:

  • uploads the channel's own videos and Shorts and sets their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions and playlists;
  • schedules publication dates;
  • reads public comments on the channel's videos and the channel's own statistics to plan future episodes.

The application is not offered to the public and acts only on the channel that its owner authorizes.
